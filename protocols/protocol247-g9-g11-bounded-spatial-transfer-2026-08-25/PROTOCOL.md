# Protocol 247: bounded G9/G11 outer-tube spatial transfer

## Question

Protocol 244 resolved a dense G10 outer marginal tube and Protocol 246 showed that its local
balance diagnostics are consistent under a half-timestep replay.  This prospective study asks
whether the late outer-tube geometry, principal stability classification, negative inward null
expansion, area increase, and spacelike causal signature transfer to the adjacent G9 and G11
spatial grids.

This is not a new parent solve.  It evolves the authenticated Protocol 228 repaired-parent G9
and G11 step-32 states with the unchanged full timestep and inherited evolution operator.

## Frozen schedule

- grids: G9 and G11;
- full timestep: `3.125e-5`;
- authenticated start: step 32;
- archived checkpoints and outer leaves: steps 43--48;
- causal-signature centers: steps 44--47;
- terminal bitwise and profile control: step 48;
- surface continuation order: 48 down to 43;
- inward-expansion stencils: widths 5, 7, and 9.

The six-leaf interval is a bounded late-time transfer study.  It is not a new dense formation
history and cannot establish a continuum convergence order.

## Ordered acceptance

Each new grid must independently pass the inherited surface admission, outward-stability,
negative-inward-expansion, strict-area-increase, and resolved-spacelike-signature gates.  Its
step-48 evolved state must be bitwise equal to the published Protocol 228 checkpoint, and its
terminal profile must satisfy the inherited absolute profile controls.

At every matched step 43--48, both adjacent pairs G9--G10 and G10--G11 must have less than 1%
relative transfer in one-sided area, equivalent-area radius, proper meridional length, and the
two radial endpoints.  The principal stability class must agree and each adjacent principal
eigenvalue difference must be below 10% relative or 0.02 absolute, exactly following Protocol
228.  All three grids must have a fully resolved uniformly spacelike signature at steps 44--47.

## Meaning

A pass establishes bounded three-grid spatial transfer of the late outer marginal-tube
observables and authorizes a separate archive-only balance-transfer calculation.  A failure
identifies which local, control, geometry/stability, or causal-signature gate did not transfer.

No outcome authorizes a continuum dynamical-horizon theorem, event horizon, connected topology,
source ownership, or an edit to the submitted paper.
