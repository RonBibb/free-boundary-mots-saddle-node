# Targeted citation audit — expanded capped-MOTS manuscript

Date: 2026-08-24

Scope: primary-source search for the load-bearing claims in `main.tex` and
`supplement.tex`. This is a citation-completeness audit, not a formal priority
or exhaustive novelty audit.

## Search domains

The search covered:

- Randall–Sundrum black holes, static localization, and dynamical collapse;
- Goldberger–Wise stabilization with scalar backreaction;
- MOTS stability operators, principal eigenvalues, marginally trapped tubes,
  and loss of strict stability;
- numerical MOTS branch creation in black-hole evolutions;
- saddle-node, pitchfork, and transcritical MOTS bifurcations;
- higher-dimensional MOTSs;
- free-boundary and capillary MOTSs, including higher-dimensional extensions;
- Israel junction conditions and generalized-harmonic evolution;
- pseudo-arclength continuation and fold normal-form diagnostics.

Queries included combinations of “braneworld,” “marginally outer trapped
surface,” “free boundary,” “capillary,” “higher dimensions,” “bifurcation,”
“saddle-node,” “pseudo-arclength,” and “generalized harmonic.” Searches were
run against arXiv and publisher or institutional metadata, with DOI records
used to verify publication details where available.

## Load-bearing additions

The following references were missing from the prior bibliography and have
been added where their content supports a specific statement:

1. **DeWolfe, Freedman, Gubser, and Karch (2000)** — nonlinear scalar–gravity
   realization of a stabilized warped fifth dimension. DOI:
   <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.62.046008>.
2. **Figueras and Wiseman (2011)** — large localized RSII black holes, paired
   with the existing small-hole citation. DOI:
   <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.107.081101>.
3. **Andersson, Mars, and Simon (2008)** — the full non-self-adjoint MOTS
   stability operator, principal-eigenvalue criterion, and local MOTT
   continuation. arXiv: <https://arxiv.org/abs/0704.2889>.
4. **Andersson, Mars, Metzger, and Simon (2009)** — MOTS evolution when the
   principal eigenvalue vanishes under a genericity condition. DOI:
   <https://doi.org/10.1088/0264-9381/26/8/085018>.
5. **Booth, Hennigar, and Pook-Kolb (2021), Part I** — the companion paper
   introducing the axisymmetric shooting method and interpreting the stability
   operator as the Jacobi equation for MOTS families. DOI:
   <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.104.084083>.
6. **Paetz and Simon (2013)** — stable marginally trapped surfaces in higher
   dimensions and their Kaluza–Klein lift/projection behavior. DOI:
   <https://doi.org/10.1088/0264-9381/30/23/235005>.
7. **Lee (2025)** — recent capillary-MOTS rigidity results with an explicit
   higher-dimensional extension. arXiv:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.16617>.
8. **Israel (1966)** — original thin-shell junction formalism. DOI:
   <https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02710419>.
9. **Pretorius (2005)** — generalized-harmonic numerical-relativity
   formulation. DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1088/0264-9381/22/2/014>.
10. **Keller (1977)** — canonical pseudo-arclength continuation reference for
    nonlinear bifurcation problems.
11. **Kuznetsov (2004)** — standard fold normal form, simple-kernel,
    transversality, quadratic nondegeneracy, and square-root scaling context.
    DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4757-3978-7>.

## Relevant works considered but not added

- Additional black-hole/black-string phase-diagram papers were not added
  because the present manuscript does not claim a phase diagram or topology
  transition.
- General dynamical-horizon reviews were not added because the paper stops at
  slice-wise free-boundary MOTSs and does not infer a global or causal horizon.
- Further free-boundary minimal-surface and capillary-rigidity papers were not
  added when they did not bear on the implemented MOTS boundary operator,
  higher-dimensional setting, or bifurcation claim.
- More generalized-harmonic and spectral-method references were not added
  because the manuscript reports a specific implementation rather than a new
  formulation or numerical algorithm.

## Priority-search outcome

The targeted searches found established literatures for each ingredient:
braneworld horizons, higher-dimensional MOTSs, free-boundary/capillary MOTSs,
and MOTS saddle-node bifurcations. They did **not** locate an exact prior result
that numerically resolves a saddle-node of brane-terminating free-boundary
MOTSs in an explicitly stabilized compact two-brane evolution.

That absence is useful but not sufficient for a priority claim. The manuscript
should retain its current no-“first” wording until a separate, documented
novelty audit searches citation networks and the higher-dimensional numerical
relativity literature more exhaustively.

## Audit conclusion

After these additions, the bibliography covers the logical ancestry of the
paper’s model, boundary formulation, MOTS operator, continuation method, and
fold classification. No reference was added merely to increase bibliography
size; each new citation either supports a load-bearing statement or closes a
recognizable dimensional or mathematical-literature gap.
